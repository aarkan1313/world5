## What shipped

Two focused fixes for two distinct artifacts the user flagged after
Phase 4.10 closed.

### 4.11.a — Removed heightmap [0,1] clamp (V-shape with straight edges)

**Bug**: walking demo showed a V-shape depression with crisp straight-
edge boundaries inside the depression — looked like a notch carved
into the snow with geometric edges that don't match natural noise.

**Root cause**: `TerrainWorld._update_ring_height_array` line 535
clamped the encoded height to `[0, 1]` via
`normalized = clamp((h / amp) * 0.5 + 0.5, 0.0, 1.0)`. With kernel
`amplitude = 50m`, any fBm noise peak/trough beyond ±50m was
flattened to the clamp boundary, producing flat plateaus at exactly
±50m with sharp level-set edges where the noise crossed the clamp.
Visible in screenshots as the V-shape's flat bottom + straight-line
crests.

**Fix**: removed the clamp. `Image.FORMAT_RF` (32-bit float) stores
arbitrary values; shader decode `(h - 0.5) * 2 * height_scale`
handles out-of-[0,1] values correctly. Bilinear interpolation between
in-range and out-of-range samples remains well-defined.

**Files**: `engine/scripts/terrain/TerrainWorld.gd:533-538`.

### 4.11.b — Tightened walking_demo slot selector bands (white patches)

**Bug**: irregular off-white patches scattered across near-flat snow.
Not chunk-aligned, not tile-aligned — patches of varying shape and
size on otherwise-uniform ground.

**Root cause**: the alpine biome catalog's slot bands had wide
overlap with aggressive crossfades. Mid-slot's slope window was
`[10°, 45°]` with an 8° band → mid was non-zero starting at just 2°
slope. Most "flat" snow has 0-10° natural micro-variation, so mid
slot bled into ground at any tiny slope variation, mixing in the
mid slot's lichen-moss texture as visible patches over the firn-snow
base.

**Fix**: tightened bands in `engine/worlds/walking_demo/biome_catalog.json`:

| Slot | Old elev_m | Old slope_deg | Old bands | New elev_m | New slope_deg | New bands |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| ground | [-50, 5] | [0, 20] | 8/5 | [-50, 15] | [0, 25] | 5/3 |
| mid | [-15, 30] | [10, 45] | 10/8 | [-5, 35] | [25, 50] | 5/4 |
| rock | [10, 50] | [30, 90] | 12/10 | [15, 60] | [45, 90] | 5/5 |

Math at flat 5° slope (typical foreground):
- Pre-fix: ground=1.0 + mid=0.32 → 24% mid bleed
- Post-fix: ground=1.0 + mid=0.0 → pure ground

Slot transitions now have clear thresholds: mid kicks in around 21°
slope, rock around 40°. Crossfade bands stay smooth but compact (3-5
units instead of 8-12). The "all three slots are exercised on
visible terrain" intent of the original wider bands is preserved
because the kernel does produce slopes >25° on real cliff features.

**Files**: `engine/worlds/walking_demo/biome_catalog.json`.

## What's load-bearing post-4.11

- **Height encoding is now unclamped.** Producers must still write
  `h / amp / 2 + 0.5` (not raw `h`); shader decode assumes this
  centered-and-scaled form. If a future kernel emits heights very
  far past ±amplitude, GPU bilinear interpolation of extreme floats
  could in theory lose precision — not observed in practice for the
  ±2× amplitude range fBm produces.
- **Walking_demo catalog bands are tuned to demonstrate slot
  transitions on the kernel's actual terrain shape.** Future world
  bundles with different kernels need their own tuning; copying
  walking_demo's bands to e.g. a flat-coastal biome would leave mid
  + rock slots inactive (no slopes > 25° in flat terrain).
- **Phase 6 forest catalog** will need its own band sweep —
  forest biomes typically have less elevation variation than alpine,
  so mid/rock bands may need lower thresholds to activate.
